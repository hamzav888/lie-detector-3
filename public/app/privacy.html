Tellmeter

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

The short version: Tellmeter runs entirely on your device. It has no server, no account and no analytics. No video frame, face landmark, score or setting is ever transmitted anywhere. There is nothing for us to see, sell or hand over, because nothing reaches us.

What the app accesses

  • Camera. Tellmeter needs the front camera to measure facial signals. Frames are analysed in memory, one at a time, and discarded immediately. Nothing is recorded to disk and nothing is uploaded. The camera is only active while the app is open and in the foreground.
  • Nothing else. No microphone, location, contacts, photos, health data, advertising identifier or device identifier is requested or used.

What is stored on your device

Two things, both local and both optional to keep:

  • Settings — your timing, sound, haptics and overlay preferences.
  • Saved readings — the label you typed, the resulting index, the per-signal numbers and a timestamp. Never any image or video.

These live in the app's private storage. Delete them any time with Settings → Sessions → Delete all, or by deleting the app. They are not included in any cloud backup we control, and we have no access to them.

Data we collect

None. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising network and no third-party service of any kind embedded in this app. The face model and all its code ship inside the app bundle, so the app makes no network requests during normal use.

Sharing

If you tap Share summary, the app hands a plain-text summary of your saved readings to the operating system share sheet, and you choose where it goes. Nothing is shared unless you initiate it.

Children

Tellmeter is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

What Tellmeter is not

Tellmeter measures visible signs of stress — blink rate, gaze movement, brow and mouth tension, head and body motion — relative to a baseline it learns from you. It is an entertainment product.

It is not a lie detector, not a polygraph, not a medical or psychological device, and not evidence of anything about any person. Stress is not deception. Do not use it to make decisions about anyone.
