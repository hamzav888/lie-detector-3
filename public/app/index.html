    About

    What's being measured

    It learns your normal first

    Twenty calm seconds build a personal baseline from six facial and motion signals. Everything after that is measured as drift from that baseline — not against some average stranger.

    One signal never convicts

    A shrug, a scratch, a passing cloud — any single spike gets discounted hard. The index only climbs when several independent signals move together, and it's pulled back toward neutral whenever the camera read is poor.

    Nothing leaves this device

    The face model runs offline, inside the app. No frame, no landmark and no score is ever uploaded — there's no account and no server. Readings you save live in local storage and you can wipe them any time.

    This is entertainment. It measures visible stress and nervousness, which is genuinely informative — but stress is not proof of lying. Calm liars and anxious honest people both exist. Don't use it to make decisions about anyone.
